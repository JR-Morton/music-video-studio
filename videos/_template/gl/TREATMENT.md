## The idea
- **Logline:** one sentence. What is the video about, and how does it turn?
- **World:** setting, a small palette, light, and how the colour changes across the song (the colour arc).
- **Cast:** who or what is on screen, what each looks like, and how each one moves (motion is character).
- **Motif:** the image that recurs and pays off (opening and ending rhyme).
- **Type:** how the lyric is set (every plate stages its own lyric; no subtitles).

## Plates
For each section: the shots (cut on bars or on sung words, never hard-coded seconds), the transition in, what's seen,
the EVENT, the camera, the lyric treatment, and its reads (what the viewer must understand, in order). Big energy =
big visuals. Check that no transition cuts off an animation or a word, and that no text appears before it's sung.
